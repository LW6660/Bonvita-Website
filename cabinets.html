Dealer Assembly Guide

Cabinet Assembly & Mounting Workflow

Step-by-step instructions for quick, precise RTA framing and soft-close hardware attachment.

01

Unbox & Inspect

Lay out side panels, backings, and face frames on a non-abrasive surface. Verify all soft-close hinges, cam locks, and dowels are present.

02

Box Assembly

Apply wood glue along tongue-and-groove joints. Lock side panels into the face frame using pre-installed cam lock systems or pocket screws.

03

Install Hinges & Slides

Attach soft-close drawer glides along pre-drilled interior pilot holes. Clip 6-way adjustable hinges onto door leaves.

04

Level & Secure

Place cabinet units onto level shims, secure boxes together through side stiles, and anchor directly into wall studs.
