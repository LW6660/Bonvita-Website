Slab Handling & Seam Setup

Essential guidelines for cutting, seaming, and securing quartz & stone surfaces.

01

Site Templating

Measure base cabinets carefully, verifying levelness and wall squareness. Allow minimum 1.5" overhang for finished edges.

02

Substrate & Cutouts

Install 3/4" plywood sub-tops where support is needed. Cut sink and cookout openings with diamond blades using continuous water cooling.

03

Seam Joining

Apply color-matched two-part epoxy resin to panel joints. Use seam setters to clamp tight until cured, then polish flush.

04

Adhesive Anchoring

Apply 100% silicone bead along cabinet top perimeters. Lower stone slabs straight down into position—do not slide.
