Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast do orders ship?

Standard products and cabinetry dispatch from our Canadian warehouse within 2 to 5 business days.

Are products shipped flat-pack or assembled?

We offer flexible fulfillment options. You can order easy-to-assemble flat-pack boxes to save on shipping and storage, or fully pre-assembled cabinetry ready for immediate installation.

What materials are used in your cabinetry?

Our cabinetry features multi-layer solid plywood core construction with zero cheap particleboard or filler materials, ensuring long-term structural durability.

What does the 10-year warranty cover?

Our 10-year warranty covers structural integrity, manufacturing defects, and hardware performance under normal commercial or residential use.

Do you deliver across Canada?

Yes, we arrange direct freight delivery and job-site fulfillment for dealer and contractor partners across Canada.

How do I become a dealer or get wholesale pricing?

You can reach out directly via our contact form or call our team. We provide spec sheets, catalog access, and transparent pricing structures for registered trade partners.
